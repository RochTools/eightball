How to play

Controls

  • Aim — slide the line at the bottom left or right for slow, very fine aiming. To turn quickly, drag anywhere on the table.
  • Power + shoot — pull the round knob along the bar at the top. The further you pull, the harder the shot. Let go to shoot, or slide it back to the start to cancel.
  • Cue ball in the pocket — drag it back, but only behind the head line (where the break starts).
  • Keyboard — ← / → aim, ↑ / ↓ power, Space to shoot.

Rules

  • Player 1 breaks from behind the head string. The table is open until someone legally pockets one — balls 1–7 are Full, balls 9–15 are Half.
  • Pocket one of your own balls without fouling and you shoot again.
  • Fouls: scratching the cue ball, hitting nothing, hitting an opponent's ball (or the 8) first, or pocketing an opponent's ball. The opponent gets 2 shots.
  • Clear your group, then pocket the 8-ball to win. If the 8 goes in early it is put back in its rack spot (foul, 2 shots). Scratching while pocketing the 8 on the last shot loses the game.
