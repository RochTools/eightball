Put your mp3 files in this folder. A missing file keeps the built-in synth sound.

  shot.mp3      - the cue hits the white ball      (alias: cue.mp3)
  ball.mp3      - ball touches ball                (alias: click.mp3)
  foul.mp3      - wrong ball / foul                (alias: wrong.mp3)
  gameover.mp3  - game over                        (alias: win.mp3)

Optional extras:
  cushion.mp3   - ball hits the rail               (alias: rail.mp3)
  pocket.mp3    - ball drops into a pocket

Keep ball/shot/cushion sounds short (under half a second).
